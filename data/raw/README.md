<h3>Technical Support Dataset.</h3>

Analyzing Technical Support KPIs for Enhanced Efficiency and Resolution

About this file:

This dataset contains detailed records of customer support tickets, providing insights into the performance and efficiency of a technical support team. Each record represents a unique support interaction, with various attributes capturing the status, handling process, and resolution of tickets. Key fields track ticket urgency, agent involvement, support channels, and compliance with Service Level Agreements (SLAs), allowing for in-depth analysis of customer service performance.

Key metrics include ticket volume trends, first response and resolution times, agent performance, and customer satisfaction scores. Additionally, geographical data such as the country of origin and coordinates (latitude, longitude) enables analysis of ticket distribution across regions. The dataset serves as a comprehensive resource for evaluating key performance indicators (KPIs) within a technical support center.
